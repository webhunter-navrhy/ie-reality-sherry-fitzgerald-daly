Description

River Cottage is a charming and beautifully renovated 1 bedroom cottage, dating back to the early 1900s.The accommodation extends to approximately 51 sqm and comprises an inviting open-plan living, kitchen and dining area. The living room features an impressive double-height ceiling with exposed wooden beams, creating a wonderful sense of space and light. The original fireplace, with its natural oak beam and 25 kw wood-burning stove, provides a warm and characterful focal point.

The loft bedroom benefits from a Velux window, which floods the space with natural light. Located just off the kitchen is a versatile guest room, which could be used as additional sleeping accommodation, a home office, study or hobby room, depending on the owner's requirements.A number of renovation and improvement works were carried out in 2022/23, thoughtfully combining the character and charm of the original cottage with modern comforts.The property is complemented by a 14 sqm workshop and 16 sqm shed, providing excellent additional space for storage, hobbies or a range of practical uses. The workshop roof was replaced in 2023 and the shed benefits from a concrete floor and double-height ceiling.The cottage enjoys approximately 1.13 acres of mature gardens, woodland and paddock, offering privacy and a peaceful rural setting.

River Cottage would make an ideal holiday home or peaceful rural retreat, combining traditional cottage character with a convenient location close to Kenmare.The cottage is situated approximately 8 km from the heritage town of Kenmare, where there is an excellent selection of shops, cafés, restaurants and local amenities, while still enjoying the tranquillity and privacy of a countryside setting.
